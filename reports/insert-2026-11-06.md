# Insert measurements, 2026-11-06

Host: Ryzen 7 5800X, WD_BLACK SN850X NVMe (XFS root), SQLite 3.54.0 (WAL, synchronous=NORMAL, secure_delete=ON), GCC 16.2.1 -O3. Workload: `build/bench_insert STORE 100000 [batch]`, 100,000 memories of about 256 bytes of placeholder tokens (random 5-hex-digit words; near-worst case for the full-text index because almost every token is a new term). Fresh store per run. Store size 78,196,736 B in every variant.

The FTS5 insert trigger is 81.5 % of insert time (about 53 us per record, about 1.3 us per token); the three secondary indexes under 2 %; validation, SHA-256, dedup lookup, row insert and change log together about 11 us per record. To repeat on real text once importers exist.
